Business Development Plan

Pathirakozhi Kiosk Concept & Strategic Expansion.

1. Executive Summary

Our vision is to introduce a Compact Kiosk Model at Pathirakozhi, inspired by the high-traffic, quick-service points seen at major retail hubs like Jayalakshmi and Pothys. The goal is to deliver high-quality, specialized "grab-and-go" food with minimal operational costs.

2. The Concept: "The Pathirakozhi Special Kiosk"

Unlike a full-scale restaurant, this small-format kiosk focuses on speed, taste, and efficiency.

  • Format: Low-footprint, aesthetically designed kiosk.
  • Menu Strategy: A curated selection of high-demand items including:
    • Signature Shawarma & Rolls
    • Specialty Shavayi
    • Gourmet Burgers
  • Unique Selling Point (USP): Utilizing our signature "Pathirakozhi" recipes to offer a taste that can't be replicated by competitors.

3. Operational Advantages

  • Low Capital Expenditure (CAPEX): Minimal construction and interior costs compared to a traditional dine-in setup.
  • Lean Staffing: Designed to be managed by a small, highly efficient team (2-3 staff members), significantly reducing monthly salary overheads.
  • High Inventory Turnover: Focusing on a limited menu ensures faster service and reduces food wastage.

4. Market Potential & Location Strategy

  • High Visibility: Following the "Jayalakshmi Model," the kiosk will be positioned to capture footfall from shoppers and commuters.
  • Impulse Buying: By offering quick bites like rolls and burgers, we tap into the "hunger on the go" segment.
  • Scalability: Once the Pathirakozhi model is proven successful, this kiosk format can be easily replicated in other high-traffic zones.

5. Action Plan for Success

To ensure this plan is viable, our next steps include:

  • Site Finalization: Identifying the exact spot for Pathirakozhi with maximum visibility.
  • Kiosk Design: Creating a modern, hygienic, and attractive kiosk structure.
  • Cost Analysis: Finalizing the exact budget for equipment and initial raw materials.
  • Trial Run: Launching with a limited menu to test customer response and optimize the workflow.
